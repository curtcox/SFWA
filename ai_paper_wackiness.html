The Pareto frontier of AI paper wackiness

Every paper plotted by how long ago it was published (log scale) versus a wackiness score (0–10), independently scored by Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. Where they disagree, a vertical bar shows the range; the dot marks the mean. The dashed line traces the running maximum — papers that, at the moment of publication, were wackier than anything before. Wackiness is a subjective score combining: how absurd the title sounds, how weird the method is, how surprising the conclusion, and how much it reads like "wait, they actually did this?" Older papers are scored relative to the expectations of their era. Some papers were scored by only one or two models — the hover card shows which. ChatGPT and Gemini suggested several additions (ELIZA, Simulators, SolidGoldMagikarp, Towards Monosemanticity, Refusal Direction, The AI Scientist) that were incorporated.
